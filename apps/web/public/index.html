03 / HOW A SERVICE WORKS

One service.
One clear journey.

Any provider can set the offer and put money behind the promise. A customer chooses a live carbon credit. RouteLock Agent checks the request, then records the public retirement proof before the provider is paid.

X LAYER / CARBON RETIREMENTLIVE

Retire a real carbon credit.

01
Any provider backs the offer

Any wallet publishes the price, terms and available supply; its first offer registers it automatically. The provider then deposits enough USD₮0 into RouteLock to cover every promise.

02
Customer chooses the credit

The customer connects a wallet, chooses a live credit and submits their name and retirement message. No payment moves before the request is checked.

03
The AI agent checks and retires

RouteLock Agent reviews the request against live carbon inventory, provider terms and backing. If approved, the issuer-side relay retires the credit and receives a public certificate.

04
X Layer records the result

The certificate and payment result are written into the service record. The provider is paid only after that proof is present; otherwise the customer is protected.
